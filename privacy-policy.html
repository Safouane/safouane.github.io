Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

This policy applies to these free Android apps published on Google Play by S1Prods ("we"):

  • Sudoku: Classic Number Puzzle (com.saf1.sudoku)
  • Sleep Sounds - White Noise (com.saf1.sleepsounds)
  • Loan & Mortgage Calculator (com.saf1.loancalc)
  • Flags & Capitals: World Quiz (com.saf1.flagsquiz)
  • Document & Text Scanner (com.saf1.docscanner)

Data we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, location or contacts. The apps have no analytics and no servers of our own. Everything you create in them (games and progress, statistics, saved mixes, loan calculations, scanned documents, settings) is stored only on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Advertising

The apps are free and show ads served by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process device identifiers (such as the Android advertising ID), your IP address and an approximate location derived from it, and information about how you interact with ads. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent before personalized ads are shown, using Google's consent tool. You can change your choice at any time from the app's settings ("Privacy settings"). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Android settings.

Camera and documents (Document & Text Scanner)

Scanning uses Google's on-device document scanner and text recognition. Images and recognized text are processed on your device and are not uploaded by us. A document leaves your device only when you choose to share or export it.

Crash and performance data

Google Play may share anonymous crash and performance reports with us (Android vitals), as allowed by your device settings.

Children

The apps are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
